Three Pathways to AI on IBM Z

Not every organization wants to build AI models from the ground up. IBM Z supports three distinct adoption paths depending on your team's expertise, timeline, and requirements:

# Pathway Who It's For What You Get
1 Turnkey / ISV Solution Organizations that want a proven, ready-to-deploy solution with minimal internal AI expertise required A fully packaged AI application from IBM or a partner ISV — install, configure, and run
2 Deploy an Existing Model Teams that already have a trained model and need to operationalize it on IBM Z IBM Z model serving infrastructure (MLz, RHOAI, TF Serving, Triton) to score models in production
3 Build Your Own Model Data science teams who need full control and have proprietary data or unique requirements The full AI lifecycle on IBM Z — prepare data, train, optimize, deploy, serve, and govern

This page covers Pathway 1. For Pathways 2 and 3, see the AI Lifecycle section.

IBM SQL Data Insights Pro for Z

IBM SQL Data Insights Pro for Z is an AI-powered analytics solution for IBM Z that helps users explore and analyze Db2 for z/OS data using natural language. It allows business users and data analysts to query data without requiring deep knowledge of SQL or underlying database structures.

Unlike AI lifecycle tools used to prepare data, train models, and manage deployments, SQL Data Insights Pro is a packaged IBM solution that applies built-in AI capabilities directly to existing Db2 for z/OS data. No model development or training workflow is required.

Key Capabilities

  • Natural language querying — Ask questions in plain English and get SQL-backed answers against Db2 for z/OS
  • AI-generated insights — Automated anomaly detection, trend analysis, and data profiling
  • No data movement — Queries run directly against z/OS data, keeping sensitive enterprise data within the Z security perimeter
  • Low barrier to entry — Business users can generate insights without SQL expertise or a data science team
  • z/OS native — Runs on z/OS with direct integration to Db2 for z/OS

ISV Ecosystem — What Are ISV Solutions?

IBM provides the underlying AI infrastructure for IBM Z, including hardware accelerators, open-source frameworks, model optimization tools, and inference runtimes. Independent Software Vendors (ISVs) build on these capabilities to deliver AI applications tailored to specific business domains such as fraud detection, anti-money laundering, and customer experience.

For organizations looking to adopt AI without building and maintaining their own models, ISV solutions can provide a direct path to production. These offerings typically include pre-built models, domain expertise, and deployment frameworks designed for specific business use cases.
