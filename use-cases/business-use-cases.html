AI on IBM Z 101

Business Use Cases

Real-world business scenarios where AI on IBM Z delivers measurable value by bringing AI processing directly to your enterprise data.

Overview

IBM Z and LinuxONE are home to the world's most critical business data — financial transactions, insurance claims, healthcare records, and more. This page highlights real-world use cases where AI on IBM Z delivers value by eliminating data movement and keeping sensitive information within the platform's security boundary.

Predictive AI

Predictive AI uses trained models to generate real-time insights from transactional data at the moment decisions need to be made.

Fraud Detection

Score payment transactions in real time at the point of processing to identify fraudulent activity before it completes — without moving data off-platform.

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

Monitor transaction flows and flag suspicious patterns consistent with money laundering, integrated directly into core banking systems on IBM Z.

Credit Risk Assessment

Score loan applications and credit decisions in real time using models trained on historical transaction and customer data residing on IBM Z.

Anomaly Detection

Identify unusual patterns in operational or transactional data — such as system events, customer behavior, or claim submissions — before they escalate.

Customer Segmentation

Cluster customers by behavior, value, or risk profile using AI models that run against enterprise data on IBM Z without requiring data exports.

Personalized Product Suggestions

Recommend relevant products or offers to customers in real time based on transaction history and behavioral signals processed on IBM Z.

Health Insurance Claims Processing

Automate and prioritize claims adjudication using AI models that flag anomalies, predict outcomes, and surface high-risk claims for review.

Generative AI

Generative AI use cases on IBM Z leverage large language models — running on the IBM Spyre Accelerator or Telum on-chip accelerator — to process documents, generate summaries, and support decision-making within enterprise workflows.

Document Processing & Intelligence

Extract, classify, and summarize information from unstructured documents such as contracts, reports, and correspondence using LLMs deployed on IBM Z.

Suspicious Activity Report Generation

After a fraud or AML alert is triggered, use an AI agent workflow to automatically gather transaction details and draft a suspicious activity report.

Medical Image Analysis

Apply deep learning models to medical imaging data — X-rays, MRIs, pathology scans — for pattern recognition and clinical decision support.

Geospatial Analysis

Process and analyze geospatial data using AI models to support logistics, risk mapping, and location-aware business decisions.
