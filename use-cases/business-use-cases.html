The AI Landscape on IBM Z

AI on IBM Z can be viewed through two primary domains: AI for Business and AI for Operations.

The AI on Z 101 page focuses on transactional AI for Business, including the AI lifecycle and the products and tools used to build AI solutions on IBM Z.

This page focuses on AI for Operations, where AI is applied to improve the management, efficiency, resilience, and security of the IBM Z environment itself. Common use cases include system optimization, issue detection, operational automation, DevOps assistance, and performance management.

The page also highlights generative AI and agentic AI capabilities on IBM Z. These capabilities can support both business and operational use cases, extending AI beyond traditional transactional workloads.

Generative AI on IBM Z

Why Organizations Use Generative AI

Generative AI helps organizations unlock value from large volumes of information by making knowledge easier to access, understand, and use. Instead of requiring users to search through documents, applications, or data sources manually, generative AI can analyze information and generate summaries, answers, recommendations, and other useful outputs.

By leveraging foundation models and large language models (LLMs), organizations can support a wide range of tasks without building a separate model for every use case.

Agentic AI on IBM Z

Why Organizations Use Agentic AI

While generative AI helps users create content and obtain information, agentic AI focuses on accomplishing tasks and achieving outcomes. Agentic systems use AI agents to reason through requests, determine the steps required to complete them, interact with tools and data sources, and execute actions across business processes and IT environments.

Organizations are adopting agentic AI to move beyond AI-assisted work toward greater automation of repetitive, complex, and multi-step activities.

Example Use Cases

  • Client-facing chatbots — Conversational agents that handle customer inquiries, routing, and resolution
  • Dev & IT advisor — AI agents that assist developers and operators with ChatOps, incident response, and system queries
  • Code generation — Agents that generate, review, and refactor code in Z environments (e.g., WCA4z)
  • Design tasks — Agents supporting design and documentation workflows

AI for IT Operations

AI for Operations focuses on using AI to optimize the IBM Z environment itself. Common use cases include system management, performance optimization, resource allocation, and operational automation rather than business transaction processing.
