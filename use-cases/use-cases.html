AI on IBM Z

Use Cases & AI Entry Points

Organizations adopting AI on IBM Z and LinuxONE start from different places — different business goals, different levels of in-house data science capability, and different amounts of existing model investment. Rather than prescribing a single path, IBM Z supports three distinct entry points for bringing AI into production.

Each entry point reflects a different answer to the question: "Where does the model come from?" The entry point you choose shapes which products, skills, and lifecycle stages are most relevant to you.

The Three AI Entry Points

Regardless of entry point, every AI solution on IBM Z follows the same end-to-end value chain — from defining the use case to delivering AI insights back to the application. The entry points differ in where you join that chain.

Turnkey Solution

Install, configure, deploy — no data science required.

IBM and its partners offer pre-built AI solutions that are ready to deploy with minimal setup. These solutions embed AI directly into the platform or provide vendor-managed models that are purpose-built for specific business domains.

When to choose this entry point

  • Your team has limited or no data science expertise
  • You need fast time-to-value with minimal build effort
  • A proven, pre-built solution already exists for your use case
  • You prefer a managed or embedded AI experience
2

Select a Model via IBM or Vendor

Leverage an existing model with IBM Z inferencing acceleration.

Choose a foundation model, open-source model, or vendor-supplied model and deploy it on IBM Z using IBM's inferencing infrastructure and hardware acceleration. No model training required — focus is on serving and integration.

When to choose this entry point

  • A suitable model already exists (IBM, open source, or vendor)
  • Your team has integration and deployment skills but not model training expertise
  • You want hardware-accelerated inferencing (Telum, Spyre) without building a model from scratch
  • You are deploying a foundation model or LLM for generative AI workloads
3

Build Your Own Model

Full AI lifecycle — from your data to your deployed model.

When no existing model meets your needs, you build one. This entry point covers the full lifecycle: find and prepare data, train a model on your enterprise data, test and validate it, optimize it for deployment, and serve it in production on IBM Z.

When to choose this entry point

  • Your use case is highly specific to your business and data
  • You have data science and machine learning expertise in-house
  • Existing models do not provide sufficient accuracy or coverage
  • You need full control over model behavior, training data, and versioning

Entry Point 1 — Turnkey Solution

Turnkey AI solutions bundle AI capability directly into the platform or product. There is no model training, no pipeline to build, and no significant data science expertise required. IBM and IBM business partners offer turnkey solutions for IBM Z across several domains.

Example Use Cases

AI-Infused IBM z/OS Operations

AI-assisted workload management predicts workload spikes and proactively adjusts system resources to optimize performance and reduce operational complexity.

SQL Data Insights

Discover hidden patterns and anomalies in Db2 data without building or managing custom ML models. AI-enhanced analytics surface business insights directly within the database environment.

ISV / Partner AI Solutions

IBM partners offer certified, pre-integrated AI solutions for fraud detection, AML, credit risk, and other financial services use cases — deployable on IBM Z with minimal configuration.

Relevant Products & Technologies

  • Machine Learning for IBM z/OS
  • SQL Data Insights for Db2
  • AI Control Interface for IBM z/OS
  • AI Systems Services for z/OS
  • IBM and ISV/partner certified AI solutions

Entry Point 2 — Select a Model via IBM or Vendor

This entry point is for teams that want to deploy an existing model on IBM Z's inferencing infrastructure — without training from scratch. The model may come from IBM, the open-source community, or a third-party vendor. The focus is on deployment, integration, and hardware-accelerated serving.

Example Use Cases

LLM Inferencing with Spyre

Deploy an open-source or IBM foundation model (e.g., Granite) on IBM Z using the Spyre Accelerator for high-throughput, low-latency generative AI inferencing close to enterprise data.

Vendor-Supplied Fraud Models

Deploy a vendor-certified predictive model for payment fraud or AML on IBM Z's inferencing stack, benefiting from Telum hardware acceleration without model development effort.

RAG with watsonx.ai

Select and serve a foundation model via watsonx.ai on IBM Z, combining it with enterprise data retrieval to power Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications.

Relevant Products & Technologies

  • Red Hat AI Inference Server (vLLM)
  • IBM Z Accelerated Serving for TensorFlow
  • Machine Learning for IBM z/OS

AI Platforms

  • watsonx.ai
  • Red Hat OpenShift AI

Hardware Acceleration

  • IBM Integrated Accelerator for AI (Telum)
  • IBM Spyre Accelerator

Entry Point 3 — Build Your Own Model

When you need a model trained on your own enterprise data, this entry point walks you through the full AI lifecycle on IBM Z. Your data science team owns the full pipeline — from raw data through to a deployed, governed model.

The Build-Your-Own Lifecycle

  1. Prepare Data — Clean, transform, and engineer features using IBM Z-native data tools and Apache Spark
  2. Train Model — Train ML or deep learning models on IBM Z or hybrid cloud, using Telum acceleration where applicable
  3. Optimize Model — Apply quantization, pruning, or compiler optimization for efficient deployment on IBM Z hardware
  4. Deploy Models — Package and deploy trained models to IBM Z production environments
  5. Serve Model — Serve real-time predictions and transaction scores at scale using your target serving runtime
  6. AI Governance — Monitor model fairness, accuracy, and compliance to meet enterprise governance requirements

Example Use Cases

Custom Fraud Detection Model

Train a model on your own transaction history to detect fraud patterns unique to your customer base and transaction mix. Deploy with Telum acceleration for real-time scoring at transaction time.

Credit Risk Scoring

Build a custom credit risk model trained on your lending portfolio data. Score loan applications in real time with low latency directly on IBM Z.

Anomaly Detection

Train an anomaly detection model on operational metrics or system logs to identify unusual patterns before they become incidents.
